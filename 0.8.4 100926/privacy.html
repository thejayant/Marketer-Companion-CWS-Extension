Marketer Companion – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026 · Version 0.8.0

Summary: Marketer Companion analyses the active page and, when you choose to connect them, reads reporting data from Google Search Console, Google Analytics 4, Google Ads, Google Business Profile, Google Merchant Center, and CallRail. Business Profile changes are sent only after you preview and explicitly submit them. Marketer Companion does not collect your data: there is no developer server to collect it to, no analytics, no error reporting and no advertising code. The developer never receives your data and does not sell it. Data is processed in the extension, in your browser, and by the services you deliberately connect.

What data we access

  • Google Search Console: Verified site list, URL Inspection results, and Search Analytics aggregates (clicks, impressions, CTR, position).
  • Google Analytics (GA4): Aggregate metrics via the Analytics Data API and property/stream metadata via the Admin API.
  • Google Ads: Accessible customer IDs and aggregate campaign, ad group, keyword, search-term, asset, cost, click and conversion reporting through the Google Ads API.
  • Google Business Profile: Authorized accounts and locations, business information, daily performance, monthly search keyword impressions, reviews including reviewer display names and replies, available Google changes, verification status, posts, media and action links. Review replies, posts, selected profile edits and photo additions are submitted only after a preview and an explicit publish action.
  • Google Merchant Center: Accessible merchant accounts and aggregate product performance, status, pricing and market-insight reports through Merchant API.
  • Your email address: Shown inside the extension UI to indicate the signed-in account (via openid email scope).
  • CallRail: When you enter your own API key, the extension requests the accounts, companies, call-summary metrics, sources, campaign attribution and filters available to that key.
  • CallRail individual calls: When you open the Calls view, the extension additionally requests the call records for the selected range. These include caller name, caller phone number, caller city and state, tracking number, call duration, answered status, device, lead status, source, campaign, keywords, landing page and referrer.
  • CallRail recordings and transcripts: When you expand a single call, the extension requests that call's detail record. If your CallRail plan includes call transcription, that record contains the machine-generated transcript, an automated call summary and call highlights, and these are displayed inside the extension. Recording audio is not downloaded or played by the extension; the recording link opens in CallRail.
  • Active-page data: Only after you open or use a page-analysis feature, the extension may inspect the current page's URL, HTML metadata, headings, links, images, canonical and robots directives, structured data, scripts, iframes, marketing-tag identifiers, and related response information needed to produce the requested audit.

How data is used

  • Google data is used only to display the Search Console and GA4 reports and diagnostics you request.
  • CallRail data is used only to display and export the attribution, call-summary, individual-call and transcript views you request.
  • Caller personal data (names, phone numbers, locations and transcript text) is displayed for the call you open and is held only in the extension's memory and the session cache described below. It is never sent anywhere other than back to CallRail's own API.
  • Active-page data is used only to create on-page SEO, structured-data, indexing, redirect and marketing-tag diagnostics inside the extension.
  • The extension sends requests directly from your browser to Google APIs, CallRail APIs, and the public page or validator you deliberately ask it to open or inspect. No data is sent to a developer-controlled analytics, advertising or profiling service.

Storage, caching and retention

  • Google authentication (default): OAuth access tokens are managed by Chrome's chrome.identity service and are not saved by the extension in persistent local storage. The signed-in email may be stored locally so the extension can show which account is connected.
  • Google authentication (multi-account mode): If multi-account sign-in has been configured for this installation, the extension uses an authorisation-code flow with PKCE instead. In that mode an OAuth refresh token and a short-lived access token are stored for each connected account in chrome.storage.local, so that you can switch accounts without signing in again. These tokens never leave your browser except in requests to Google. Removing an account, or signing out, revokes the refresh token with Google and deletes it locally.
  • CallRail credential: The API key is stored in chrome.storage.session, is available only to the extension, and is cleared when the browser session ends or when you choose Disconnect.
  • Google Ads manager setting: If supplied, the optional manager customer ID is stored in chrome.storage.session and cleared when the browser session ends or when you choose Clear manager ID. No Google Ads developer token is requested or stored.
  • Business Profile reports: Business Profile content and calculated results stay in memory. The most recent report is held in the browser's session storage so that reopening the popup does not refetch it; that storage is never written to disk, is scoped to the signed-in Google identity, and is discarded when the browser session ends. It is also cleared on sign-out and account changes. Reports are not saved to the report caches. Only user preferences and explicitly saved client-to-location identifiers are stored persistently. No review text is sent to an AI service.
  • Other report caches: Property lists, account metadata and report results from other integrations may be cached in chrome.storage.local to improve speed and reduce API requests. Report caches are limited and refreshed or replaced as you use the extension.
  • Session report cache: The reports you have loaded, including CallRail call records and any transcript you opened, are kept in chrome.storage.session so that closing and reopening the popup or side panel does not discard your work. This store is cleared automatically by Chrome when the browser session ends.
  • Preferences: Interface choices, selected properties, date ranges and filters may be stored locally until the extension is removed or the relevant setting is changed.
  • Signing out clears the extension's Google account profile, any stored Google tokens, and the Google report caches. Disconnecting CallRail clears the session key and all CallRail caches, including cached call records and transcripts. Removing the extension clears its Chrome-managed storage.

Permissions and user control

  • Google sign-in is initiated only when you click Connect Google. The extension requests openid, email, webmasters.readonly, analytics.readonly, adwords, content, and business.manage. Business Profile's scope permits management; the interface requires explicit submission for any change.
  • activeTab and scripting are used to inspect only the page you are actively viewing when you invoke an applicable feature. The extension does not request permanent access to all browsing history.
  • You can use page-level audit features without connecting Google or CallRail, and you can disconnect either service independently.

Third-party resources

  • The interface loads the Plus Jakarta Sans typeface from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com). This request is made by your browser when the extension interface is opened and is subject to Google's own privacy policy. No extension data is included in it.
  • No analytics, advertising, error-reporting or profiling service of any kind is embedded in this extension.

Google API Limited Use

Marketer Companion's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google user data is not used for advertising, credit-worthiness, lending, surveillance, or training generalised artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models, and is not sold or transferred except as necessary to provide the user-requested extension features, comply with law, or address security and abuse.
