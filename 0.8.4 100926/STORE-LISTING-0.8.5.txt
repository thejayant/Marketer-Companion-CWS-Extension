5. DETAILED DESCRIPTION  (max 16,000)
-------------------------------------
Marketer Companion puts the reports you already pay attention to in one place,
inside the browser, without a middleman server.

Sign in with your own Google account and the extension reads the properties,
accounts and locations that account can already see. Nothing is uploaded
anywhere: the browser talks directly to Google's APIs and to CallRail, and the
results stay on your machine.

WHAT IT REPORTS

• Blended report — Search Console, GA4 and CallRail describing one slice of
  traffic at a time, with period-over-period comparison and a daily trend. Every
  source agrees on which slice that is: each segment defines itself twice, once
  for the GA4 channel group and once for the CallRail call source, so sessions,
  key events and calls always describe the same audience.

• Search Console — clicks, impressions, CTR and average position by query, page,
  country and device, plus URL inspection: coverage state, canonical, last crawl
  and robots status for any page.

• Google Analytics 4 — 30 dimensions and 18 metrics against the Analytics Data
  API, key events, and CSV export.

• Google Ads — campaign, ad group, keyword, search term, asset and conversion
  reporting through the Google Ads API, with account timezone and currency,
  totals, trends, comparisons and CSV export.

• Google Business Profile — visibility, website clicks, call clicks and
  direction requests across one location or a whole portfolio; month-by-month
  search keywords; review triage with reply drafting; and a content inventory of
  posts and photos.

• Merchant Center — product performance and status, price competitiveness,
  price insights, best sellers and competitive visibility.

• CallRail — call volume, call sources, individual records and transcripts.

• On-page audit — title, meta description, headings, links and images for the
  page you are looking at. No API and no sign-in needed.

• Structured data — JSON-LD, Microdata and RDFa on the current page, rendered as
  readable cards rather than raw markup.

BUILT FOR PEOPLE RUNNING SEVERAL CLIENTS

Save a client once and the extension remembers which Search Console property,
GA4 property, CallRail company and Business Profile locations belong to them.
Switching client switches every panel at once. Mappings are scoped to the Google
identity that saved them, so one browser profile can hold several accounts
without their data mixing.

HONEST NUMBERS

The reports are built to be defensible in front of a client:

• Partial data is labelled partial. If three of six locations fail to load, you
  are told so above the totals, and the totals are never presented as a complete
  portfolio figure.
• Missing days are gaps, not zeros.
• Privacy-thresholded keyword counts stay as "<5" and are never substituted into
  an exact total.
• A percentage change with no baseline says so instead of showing infinity.
• Impressions are labelled as the sum of platform and device metrics, not as
  unique people.
• Call clicks are described as button clicks, not as answered calls or leads.

WHERE YOUR DATA GOES

Nowhere. There is no developer server, no analytics, no error reporting and no
advertising code. Google tokens are handled by Chrome's identity service or kept
locally; your CallRail API key is held for the browser session only. Report
content lives in the open interface and is cleared when you sign out or switch
accounts.

REQUIREMENTS

• A Google account with access to the properties you want to report on.
• For Google Ads, Merchant Center and Business Profile, the Google Cloud project
  behind the sign-in needs those APIs enabled and the relevant access approved.
• CallRail is optional and needs an API key from your own CallRail account.

Designed and built by Jayant.
